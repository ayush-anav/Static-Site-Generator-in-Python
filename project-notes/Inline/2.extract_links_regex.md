# Extract Links
#### `we are using regex for this one`

Regex by default in python can be used via:
**`import re`**

And a simple method is:
**`re.findall(*pattern*, *text*)`**

---
## 1. Extraction for images
#### images: `r"!\[([^\[\]]*)\]\(([^\(\)]*)\)"`

---

## 2. Links
#### regular links: `r"(?<!!)\[([^\[\]]*)\]\(([^\(\)]*)\)"`

---

### Learning Moment: 
###### Greedy vs Non-greedy matching

Greedy vs non-greedy matching is fun to think about.  
##### `Think of greedy as pushing the rules of your regex SO FAR and including as much as it can without violating it.`

Non-greedy can be thought of just 
##### `strictly matching what you give your pattern as and at the last place it has to match, it will stop, no pushing the rules or anything.`
